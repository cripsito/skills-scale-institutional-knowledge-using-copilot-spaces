# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project-management lifecycle that moves work through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement. Teams start with a one-pager to define the problem, outcomes, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, initial risks, and resource needs. Once stakeholders align on priority and team capacity, initiatives move into planning with a prioritized backlog, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a clear Definition of Done.

Delivery is organized around clear ownership and cross-functional collaboration. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, documentation, and stakeholder alignment; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure impact; Developers design, implement, test, and review solutions; QA validates acceptance criteria and release readiness. Work is tracked in a shared board flow (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done) to keep status visible and actionable.

Communication and risk management follow a regular cadence and a defined escalation path. Daily standups and weekly delivery syncs surface progress, blockers, and dependencies, while recurring stakeholder updates capture progress, next steps, risks, and decisions needed. Risks are maintained in a living register with impact, likelihood, owner, mitigation, and status, then escalated from team triage to PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as business impact grows; security incidents follow the dedicated incident runbook and on-call path.

Quality assurance and release practices are embedded throughout delivery rather than deferred to the end. Teams use explicit acceptance criteria, small reviewable pull requests where practical, CI validation (tests, linting, security scanning), and fit-for-purpose testing (unit, integration, smoke, and manual QA when needed). Releases require release notes, rollback planning, staging and production verification, and stakeholder communication, followed by retrospectives that convert lessons learned into owned, time-bound improvements. These documents are the shared, versioned source of OctoAcme project-management processes for Copilot Spaces and the team.
